## Release notes

Fixes for the gutter, folding and selection, found with the real-use simulation harness.

### Fixed

- **Folding with a selection** — folding a region while text inside it was selected left the selection hidden; typing then deleted text you could not see. A fully hidden selection now becomes a caret on the header line, a partly hidden one is trimmed to what stays visible, and ⇧↓ from a folded header no longer selects hidden lines one by one.
- **Text and line numbers out of step** — after Fold All (and some other redraws) the text could stay where it was while line numbers, fold markers and clicks followed a position 9–36 pt away, until the next scroll.
- **Current-line band** — after undoing a multi-line change or inserting a date the old band stayed on screen as a second band; in a wrapped paragraph the gutter painted the whole paragraph while the text painted one visual line.
- **Last empty line** — the empty line after a trailing newline, and an empty document, had no line number.
- **Column block highlight** — a column selection whose first line was shorter than the block's left edge drew no highlight at all.

### For contributors

- 15 `mouse-*` real-use scenarios: 2,288 clicks land on the clicked character; they also compare the fragment views on screen with the layout (`checkScreen`).

### Compatibility

- macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later. The DMG (`HyperEdit-<version>-universal.dmg`) contains one universal binary that runs natively on Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (x86_64) Macs.
