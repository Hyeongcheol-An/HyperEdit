## Release notes

Scrolling fixes, found with the real-use simulation harness.

### Fixed

- **First scroll after a pause** — after jumping to the end, going to a line or opening a file at its end, the first wheel step upward moved less than the wheel (or even the other way in wrapped paragraphs), and Page Up was uneven.
- **Empty band below the last line** — ⌘↓, Go to Line on the last line and End could leave the last line in the middle of the window with an empty band below; the next wheel step then jumped by that amount.
- **Find highlights and arrow keys** — a second cause of highlights disappearing when the caret moved without scrolling.

### Known limitations

- With word wrap off, large files still lay out the whole document on some operations and can pause for seconds; folded documents and long sessions can still scroll by slightly more or less than the wheel. These are being worked on separately.

### For contributors

- 13 `scroll-*` real-use scenarios measure how far an anchor paragraph moves on screen per wheel step instead of comparing document coordinates, which TextKit 2 re-estimates while scrolling.

### Compatibility

- macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later. The DMG (`HyperEdit-<version>-universal.dmg`) contains one universal binary that runs natively on Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (x86_64) Macs.
