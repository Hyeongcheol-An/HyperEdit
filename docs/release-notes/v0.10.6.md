## Release notes

The text now moves by exactly what you scroll, also in long documents and after switching tabs.

### Fixed

- **Scrolling drifted after long scrolls and after switching tabs** — after moving through about 2,000 paragraphs of one document the view could jump by hundreds of lines (line 1772 → 1129 on one wheel step in a 1.3 MB note), and after switching between tabs a wheel step of 75 pt moved the text by 0, 51 or −46 pt in every document until the app was restarted. TextKit 2 keeps the layout of at most 2,000 paragraphs and estimates the rest, and entries of replaced documents kept that table full. The editor now puts the text back where it was when a layout pass moves it, and resets the limit so the table is not flushed on every pass.
- **Lines with Hangul were 1.3 pt taller** than other lines, so composing Hangul on the last visible line re-centred the view (a jump of about 220 pt). All lines now have the same height; Hangul lines are 1.3 pt closer together than before.
- **Arrow keys at the edge of the window** — moving the caret past the top or bottom edge with the arrow keys re-centred the view by half a window. The view now scrolls only as far as needed.

### Known limitations

- The scroll bar knob can still move a few points against the scroll direction, because TextKit 2 re-estimates the document height while scrolling.
- Typing on the last visible line still re-centres the view (AppKit behaviour).
- With word wrap off, large files can still pause for seconds.

### For contributors

- The fragment cache limit is a private value of `NSTextLayoutManager`; it is only read and set when it exists, and the public-API scroll anchor works without it. `HyperTextView.adjustsFragmentCacheLimit` turns the adjustment off for tests.
- New real-use scenarios: `scroll-long-run`, `scroll-after-switch`, `scroll-anchor-only`, `scroll-arrow-edges`, `scroll-first-open-end`, `scroll-line-height`.

### Compatibility

- macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later. The DMG (`HyperEdit-<version>-universal.dmg`) contains one universal binary that runs natively on Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (x86_64) Macs.
