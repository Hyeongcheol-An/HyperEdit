## Release notes

Folded documents scroll and re-flow correctly, Fold All and Unfold All keep the line with the caret where it was, the two editors of a split now share their folds, and keys next to a fold no longer touch lines you cannot see.

### Fixed

- **Scrolling a folded document** — with many folds in a large file, wheel steps jumped by hundreds of points, End left an empty band and lines without numbers, and the document was treated as if nothing were folded. In a fully folded 1.3 MB note a wheel step took 28 ms on average with pauses of up to 1.2 s; it now takes about 8 ms without pauses.
- **Changing the layout with folds** — changing the text size, the sidebar or the window width with everything folded blocked the app for up to a second and could move the view to a different place. The line you were reading stays put and none of these changes stalls.
- **Unfold All** — Unfold All kept the top line of the window in place, so every fold between that line and the caret opened above the caret and pushed its line off the bottom of the screen (from 196 pt to 772 pt down in a 382 pt editor). The caret's line now stays at the same height. When the caret is off screen, the top line is kept as before; unfolding a single region keeps the top line as before.
- **Fold All** — Fold All kept the top line of the window, so the header that the caret moves to was pulled up by every region that folded above it. The caret's line — or, when that line is folded away, the header that takes its place — now stays at the same height (127.3 pt before and after). When the caret is not on screen, the view stays on the place you were reading.
- **Carets and column blocks on hidden lines** — a column block dragged or extended across a fold has a row on every hidden line, and Select All Matches or ⌘D can put carets inside a fold; the fold stayed closed and the next key edited lines that were not on screen. Such a fold now opens. Folding a region that has several carets inside left the hidden ones where they were — and when the main caret was among them, Fold did nothing. Every caret now moves to the end of the header line; a column block collapses to one caret.
- **Files with CR LF line ends** — folding a region with the caret inside it put the caret between the CR and the LF of the header line, so the next character typed split that line end: the document gained a lone CR and a lone LF, and the line numbers below were off by one. Several carets, ↑ across a fold onto its header and ↓ on the header of a fold that reaches the end of the document ended in the same place. The caret now stops before the CR.
- **Writing right below a folded section** — after typing on the line below a folded section and selecting part of it, the fold grew over that line and the next key replaced text that was not on screen. A line that holds a caret, a selection or a block row is no longer folded away. When the section has to open because the line you are writing now belongs to it, that line stays where it is instead of sliding down by the height of the section (below a 60-line section it left the screen).
- **Arrow keys on a wrapped folded header** — Down on the first screen line of a folded header that wraps jumped straight below the fold, so the rest of the header could not be reached with the arrow keys, and Up from a wrapped line below the fold skipped that line's upper screen lines. The caret now steps through the screen lines.
- **Undo and fold arrows** — after undo or redo in a document with nothing folded, the gutter arrows disappeared for a moment and Fold could fold lines that were not a region (lines 10–12 for a caret on line 10 after undoing two lines inserted above). Undoing a line inserted right above a region no longer removes that region's arrow.
- **Scrolling with thousands of folds** — the marker strip beside the scroll bar was redrawn on every scroll step. In a document with 15,000 folds a wheel step took 13.7–16.3 ms; it now takes 5.1 ms.
- **Very large folds** — folding 12,000 lines under one header takes 22 ms, and typing on that header 2.5 ms a key.

### Changed

- **The two editors of a split share their folds.** Folds now belong to the document instead of to one editor: folding or unfolding in one editor of a split does the same in the other. Until now each editor had its own folds. The other editor keeps the place it was showing; if its caret ends up inside a fold, the caret moves to the end of the fold's header line.
- **A key that reaches hidden text opens the fold first.** Moving or extending the selection by a character, a word (⌥→, ⇧⌥→), to the end of a line or paragraph, or by ⇧↑ / ⇧↓ opens a fold as soon as the step would reach its hidden lines, and the key then does exactly what it does in the unfolded document: ⇧⌥→ at the end of a folded header selects the first word of the first hidden line, with the fold now open. Deleting by word or line next to a fold likewise removes just what it removes in the unfolded document, and the fold opens so that you see what went. A step that only reaches the header's own line break (⇧→ at its end) leaves the fold closed, and so does a line-end key that does not move the caret (⌘→ at the end of the header). A key that shrinks a selection is judged by the end it moves, so the fold next to the other end stays closed (⇧← three times from the end of a folded header, then ⇧⌥→). ↑ and ↓ without a selection still skip over a fold. This holds for folds inside folds — every fold the step passes through opens — and for right-to-left paragraphs, where ⌥← moves forward in the text.

### Known limitations

- A fully folded screen still scrolls at two to three times the cost of an unfolded one (5.1 ms against 1.7 ms a wheel step in a document with 15,000 folded two-line regions).
- In a paragraph that mixes left-to-right and right-to-left text, ← and → are checked in both directions, so such a key can open a fold it does not reach.
- For a selection that was not made with the caret keys — a drag, for instance — the first selecting key is checked from both ends, because the editor cannot tell which end will move: the fold next to the end that stays can open.
- Lines that end with a lone CR (the classic Mac format) are not handled: folding breaks lines at LF, with or without a CR before it.
- As before, folds are kept only while the app runs, and a mouse drag across a folded header selects the hidden text.

### For contributors

- Hidden paragraphs used to be laid out as zero-height fragments. TextKit 2 honours that only for fragments it has laid out: every other range is estimated from the paragraph content, and at most 2,000 fragments stay laid out, so a 51,000 pt folded document was measured as 537,651 pt in a windowless test. Hidden paragraphs are now left out of the content storage's element enumeration (`NSTextContentStorageDelegate`'s `shouldEnumerate`), and `FoldingContentStorage` restarts the enumeration past a fold so that their elements are never created (18.3 → 9.2 ms per wheel step in the folded note). The last paragraph of the document stays enumerated, because `NSTextView` cannot size the document without it; it is hidden with a zero-height fragment and a 0.01 pt display paragraph. Fold changes are announced with `edited(.editedAttributes)` — `invalidateLayout(for:)` leaves stale height estimates above the viewport.
- Unfold All in `FoldController` wraps the change in `HyperTextView.preservingReadingPosition(preferCaret: true)`: the reading anchor is the caret line when that line is on screen, and the top line otherwise. Folding and single unfolds keep `preferCaret: false`. `mouse-foldall` measures the caret line's screen position before and after the command.
- `FoldStore` (one per content storage) holds the folds and the candidates; `FoldController` (one per editor) keeps the pills, the gutter, the commands and the reading position. When a command changes the folds, the editor that issued it anchors on its caret line for Fold All and Unfold All; when an edit or a rescan opens a fold, the editor that has the keyboard focus does.
- AppKit moves the caret through the laid-out paragraphs, so its steps jump over a closed fold. Before a caret-step command runs, `openFoldsInReach` works out from the string what the step would reach in the unfolded document and opens the folds that hide it (⇧⌥→ after the `z` of a folded `def third(z):` used to select across the closed fold, and Delete then removed the two hidden lines: 225 → 184 characters, 213 in the open document). `stepIntoFold`, the fix-up after the step, remains for the boundary cases. A step that extends the selection notes every hidden paragraph in its reach, so nested folds all open; a step without a selection opens only what hides the place the caret lands on. The reach starts from the end the previous caret step moved (`caretStepHead`; from both ends when that is not known), and a ← or → command is read in the writing direction of its paragraph (`writingDirections`: the paragraph style, otherwise the first strong character; both ways when the paragraph mixes directions).
- The end of a folded header is the end of the line's text (`FoldStore.headerEnd(before:)`), not the offset before the hidden lines: with CR LF line ends that offset lies between the CR and the LF. The caret moved out of a fold, the anchor of Fold All, the pill and the arrow keys that cross a fold use it, and a one-character step counts CR LF as one step. For lines that end with LF alone every value is the same as before. `fold-crlf` counts the CR LF pairs, lone CRs and lone LFs of the document after each step (53 issues before).
- Undo and redo do not go through `shouldChangeText`; their edits are forwarded to the fold store whenever there is something to move — folds, regions, or a range that still has to be laid out again (`tracksUndoEdits`). A range freed by an edit is kept in post-edit coordinates and moved by the later edits of the same batch; with pre-edit offsets a Replace All above and inside a fold that reached the end of the document left the last line of the file off screen.
- The multi-caret ranges are trusted in the selection watcher only when they match the native selection: in the middle of a multi-caret edit they are still pre-edit offsets while the folds have already moved.
- Real-use scenarios in `RealUse+Fold.swift` (`fold-keys`, `-edit`, `-find`, `-partial`, `-reflow`, `-giant`, `-split`, `-secret`, `-nowrap`), `RealUse+FoldGuard.swift` (`fold-delete`, `-lineends`, `-replace`, `-multi`, `-rescan`, `-enum`) and `RealUse+FoldGuard2.swift` (`fold-multicaret`, `-wordstep`, `-tall`, `-undo`, `-wrapped`, `-strip`, `-nested`, `-rtl`, `-carets-far`, `-crlf`). The guard scenarios press the same keys in the unfolded and in the folded document and compare the results. The self-test counts paragraphs against layout fragments after unfolding, so a line left out of the layout is caught. `scroll-folded` and `layout-fold` went from 100 issues to none and can go back into the full run.

### Compatibility

- macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later. The DMG (`HyperEdit-<version>-universal.dmg`) contains one universal binary that runs natively on Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (x86_64) Macs.
- Folds can no longer differ between the two editors of a split.
