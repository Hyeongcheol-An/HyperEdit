## Release notes

Changing the layout while looking at the end of a large document no longer freezes the app.

### Fixed

- **Freeze at the end of a document** — at the end of a 1.3 MB note, making the text smaller, closing the sidebar or closing the split each blocked the app for 0.47–0.81 s, and turning word wrap off at the end of a 4 MB log took 2.2 s. It happened whenever the change made the document shorter than the place you were looking at. None of them stalls over 250 ms now; the log takes 50 ms.

### For contributors

- When a reflow shrinks the document height estimate, the scroll position — still the old one — lies beyond the new end of the document. `layoutViewport()` there finds no paragraph at that height and lays out paragraph after paragraph from the start of the document, running the syntax-color validator on each: 457–694 ms in the 1.3 MB note and 5 s in the 4 MB log, against 5–7 ms at the same place when the height grows instead. `HyperTextView.enterDocumentBeforeViewportLayout` asks where the document ends by laying out its last paragraph (1 ms) and, if the view is past it, moves three screens above the end before the first viewport layout of `restoreReadingAnchor`; the existing loop then settles on the anchor. It keeps a margin because that end and the estimate TextKit uses to look a position up differ by a few hundred points.

### Compatibility

- macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later. The DMG (`HyperEdit-<version>-universal.dmg`) contains one universal binary that runs natively on Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (x86_64) Macs.
