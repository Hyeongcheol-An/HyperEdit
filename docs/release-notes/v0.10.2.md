## Release notes

Fixes for glitches seen while typing, found with the new real-use simulation harness.

### Fixed

- **Line numbers while typing** — after Return or joining lines, the status bar's Ln / Col and the gutter numbers below the caret stayed a line off until typing paused for a quarter of a second. They now follow every edit.
- **Find highlights and arrow keys** — moving the caret with the arrow keys erased the match highlights on the lines that were redrawn until the next scroll.
- **Blank lines after a large paste** — pasting a few hundred lines over a selection at the end of a document could leave the last visible lines numbered but empty until the next scroll.
- **Undo and redo out of view** — undoing or redoing a large change could leave the caret outside the window.
- **The 10,000-line boundary** — when a document grew past 9,999 lines the gutter widened, the text re-wrapped and the caret could end up off screen.

### For contributors

- `MemoDocument.liveLineIndex` is updated incrementally on every edit; the status bar, the gutter and line jumps read it. The published `lineIndex` is still the debounced rebuild.
- 18 `typing-*` real-use scenarios cover input-method composition, undo grouping, paste, snippets, multi-caret, no-wrap and the gutter-width boundary.

### Compatibility

- macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later. The DMG (`HyperEdit-<version>-universal.dmg`) contains one universal binary that runs natively on Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (x86_64) Macs.
