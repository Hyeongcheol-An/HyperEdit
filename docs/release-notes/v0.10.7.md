## Release notes

The editor keeps your place when the layout changes, and find jumps land where they should.

### Fixed

- **Keeping your place** — zooming, switching themes, toggling word wrap, line numbers, the sidebar, the preview or the split, and resizing the window re-laid the whole document while the scroll position stayed put, so a different part of the document appeared: about 1,000 lines away after ⌘= in a 1.3 MB note, 9,500 lines after turning wrap off, and the caret far outside the window when zooming at the end of a document. Now the line with the caret stays at the same height when the caret is on screen; otherwise the top line does, and a view at the end of the document stays at the end.
- **File changed by another program** — when the open file was read again, the view drifted by a few points, and with the caret off screen it jumped to the caret (50 lines away in one test). The lines you were reading stay where they were.
- **Scrolling after a width change** — after closing the preview, two wheel steps could skip 2,000 lines.
- **Find jumps and Go to Line** — clicking a distant result or going to a distant line showed a place a few hundred lines away; in documents with long paragraphs the selection could be off screen. The target line is placed a third of the way down the window.
- **Line numbers and highlights out of step** — after a theme change line numbers were drawn 29 pt below their text, and the first scroll moved less than the wheel.
- **Quick Find** — the result list did not scroll to the current match.
- **Fold All** — the header that contains the line you were reading comes to the top, and Unfold All keeps it there.

### Known limitations

- Turning word wrap off still pauses for 1.7–4.5 s in large documents, and scrolling with wrap off can show blank areas in documents with very long lines.
- In a folded document, changing the text size can still move the view.
- Typing Return on the last visible line still re-centres the view (AppKit behaviour).

### For contributors

- 18 `find-*` and `layout-*` real-use scenarios, plus `tabs-reload-view`. `HyperTextView.captureReadingAnchor` / `restoreReadingAnchor` hold a line across a reflow and hand the result to the scroll anchor; the saved view position is read from the laid-out viewport instead of a point query, which moved fragment frames after a full invalidation.

### Compatibility

- macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later. The DMG (`HyperEdit-<version>-universal.dmg`) contains one universal binary that runs natively on Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (x86_64) Macs.
